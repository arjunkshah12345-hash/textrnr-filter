# Design Context — textrnr filter

## Users & Purpose
- Creators grading night / window / street photos for social posts
- Job: drop a photo → get a soft-focus night look → download
- Emotion: quiet, cinematic, intimate — a darkroom, not a dashboard

## Brand
- Personality: nocturnal, soft, filmic
- Anti-references: purple AI SaaS, glassmorphism cards, neon glow, pill-button toolbars
- Must feel like the filter itself: amber bloom, cool shadows, grain

## Aesthetic
- Dark mode only (warm near-black, not cool navy)
- One accent: sodium amber
- Brand name is the hero; short supporting line; image is the visual plane
- No card grids; controls as a film strip / rail
